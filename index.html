Why this site exists

Numbers like income or height feel abstract until you see them next to the rest of the world. These tools aren't meant to be precise scientific instruments — they use publicly available, well-known global averages and distributions to give you a rough, honest sense of perspective in a few seconds. More comparisons are on the way.
